SCOUTING REPORT — BETHEL

Film-charted tendencies. "Offense" = Bethel with the ball; "Defense" = Bethel defending. Percentages are share of charted plays unless noted.
KEY OBSERVATIONS
Bethel on offense
  • Formation is a strong play-type indicator: Dodge & Tray ~90% run; Tag, Triple, Trips ~100% pass.
  • Run has been inefficient — 3.1 YPC despite a run-heavy early-down profile.
  • 3rd & 4th-and-long: 83–88% pass.
  • Passing is their more productive phase: 80% completions; 13% of passes explosive (16+).
Bethel on defense
  • Zone-based: Cover 4 (35%), 3 Roll (32%), 3 Sky (17%); man ~8%.
  • Has allowed a 58% offensive success rate.
  • Softest by results: 3 Sky (86% success allowed), 3 Roll (72%).
  • Explosives allowed cluster vs Cover 4 (25% of those snaps).
